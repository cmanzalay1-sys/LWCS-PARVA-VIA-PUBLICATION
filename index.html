Our Philosophy

Against the Ephemeral: The Art of Small-Batch Publishing

Founded in 2019 as an antidote to algorithmic publishing, Parva Via Publication releases no more than six titles each calendar year. Every manuscript we acquire undergoes months of line-by-line editorial dialogue, bespoke typographic setting, and tactile material curation.

We partner with family-owned offset printers and traditional binderies to produce Smyth-sewn paperbacks with French flaps and clothbound hardcovers stamped in genuine brass foil. Our texts are set in classic literary faces on heavyweight, acid-free natural cream paper that softens glare and honors the quiet intimacy of reading.

Archival Materials

Call for Manuscripts

Submit Your Work to the Press

We read unsolicited manuscripts year-round with particular attention to voice, formal precision, and intellectual urgency.

Submission Guidelines

1. Categories & Word Counts

  • Literary Fiction & Novellas: 25,000 – 85,000 words.
  • Poetry Collections: 48 – 80 pages (single poem per page).
  • Essays & Philosophy: 20,000 – 65,000 words.
  • Translations: Please include 10 pages of the source text alongside translator bio.

2. Manuscript Formatting

Submit a sample excerpt (first 30 pages for prose; 15 pages for poetry) in .PDF, .DOCX, or .RTF format. Set in 12pt serif type, double-spaced, with numbered pages and 1-inch margins.

3. Reading Windows & Response

Every submission is read by at least two senior editors. Our typical response window is 8 to 12 weeks. Simultaneous submissions are warmly welcomed—kindly notify us if placed elsewhere.
