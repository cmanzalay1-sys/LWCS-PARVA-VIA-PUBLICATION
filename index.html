About the Imprint

The Meaning of Parva Via

Parva via is a Latin phrase meaning “the small way” or “the little way” (parva = small, little, modest; via = way, road, path). Within the life of Little Way College Seminary, it carries humility and simplicity in everyday duties and studies, deliberate focus on steady reflection and craft, and the spiritual resonance of the “Little Way” (la petite voie) associated with St. Thérèse of Lisieux.
